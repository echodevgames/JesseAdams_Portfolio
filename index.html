Who I Am

I’m Jesse “Echo” Adams, a game developer who enjoys building the systems that hold a game together: gameplay logic, AI behavior, save/load flow, combat, UI structure, camera control, player feedback, and the connective code that turns scattered features into a complete experience.

My background has shaped me into a practical, adaptable developer. I’m comfortable looking at the full game, breaking features into clear tasks, organizing work across a team, and stepping into the technical work needed to keep development moving.

I approach development as both a builder and a problem-solver. Whether I’m working in Unity with C#, Unreal Engine with C++, or collaborating with artists and designers, my goal is the same: create systems that are reliable, readable, and satisfying for players to interact with.
