AI Behavior & Combat

Modular behavior tasks for patrol, pursue, and attack flow with animation-driven events, gameplay interfaces, and cleaner C++ architecture.

Overview

This case study focuses on AI behavior and combat structure in Unreal Engine 5. The work centered on turning scattered Blueprint logic into cleaner gameplay systems using behavior tasks, interfaces, and animation-driven combat events.

The goal was to make enemies easier to reason about, easier to tune, and easier to extend as new combat behaviors were added.

Highlights

  • Structured enemy behavior around patrol, pursue, and attack states
  • Converted Blueprint-heavy behavior into cleaner C++ tasks and interfaces
  • Used animation-driven events to control combat timing and hit windows
  • Separated AI decision-making from combat response logic
  • Improved maintainability by reducing duplicated behavior logic

Technical Focus
